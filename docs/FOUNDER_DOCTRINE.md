# Founder Doctrine

## Permanent statements

**Sovereign will never be the doctor.**

**The clinician decides; Sovereign makes the decision executable.**

The wrong architecture makes the model the system of record. The correct architecture makes the model a reasoning component inside a controlled system. For Sovereign, the second architecture is mandatory.

Sovereign exists to understand source evidence, structure candidates, expose uncertainty, preserve clinical intent, verify executability, coordinate authorized work, and prove completion. It may reduce friction but may not blur responsibility.

## Consequences

- Models propose; authorized domain services establish persistent state.
- Clinical judgment and Class D actions remain human.
- Clinical ambiguity is displayed as `REQUIRES CLINICAL DECISION`.
- No AI agent invents identity, facts, negation, intent, authority, or completion.
- No side effect bypasses verification, policy, authority, durable execution, confirmation, and audit.
- Product metrics never reward speed that degrades safety or encourages rubber-stamping.
- The system must remain operable in safe manual/degraded mode.
