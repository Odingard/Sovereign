# Product Principles

1. The clinician decides; Sovereign makes the decision executable.
2. Source before synthesis: material assertions must be traceable.
3. Discussion, intent, authorization, action, confirmation, and completion are different facts.
4. Unknown remains unknown. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
5. One controlled mutation path and one controlled external-execution path.
6. Human responsibility remains visible; minimize unnecessary clicks, never clinical responsibility.
7. Completion means the clinical/operational objective is evidenced, not that software ran.
8. Durable workflows survive restarts, days-long waits, duplicates, and provider failure.
9. Core semantics are provider-independent; vendors belong behind adapters.
10. Patient safety, identity integrity, privacy, and explainability are release gates.
11. Local/synthetic proof precedes cloud integration and real PHI.
12. Every consequential decision can be reconstructed from evidence, intent, authority, state, and audit.
